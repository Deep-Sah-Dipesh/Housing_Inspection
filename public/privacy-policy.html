Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Housing Inspection ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the Housing Inspection mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide inspection management services, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • User account identifiers
  • Device identifiers
  • Approximate location
  • Precise location
  • Inspection photos
  • Inspection videos
  • Audio recordings when uploaded by users
  • Files and documents related to inspections
  • Inspection notes, reports, and other user-provided information

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Create, store, and manage inspection reports
  • Upload and retrieve inspection media
  • Associate inspections with inspection locations
  • Provide application functionality
  • Improve user experience and personalization
  • Maintain platform security and reliability

3. Location Information

The application may collect approximate and precise location information when necessary for inspection-related activities. Location data is used to associate inspection records with physical properties and inspection sites.

4. Media and File Uploads

Users may voluntarily upload photos, videos, audio recordings, documents, and other inspection-related files. These files are stored securely and used solely to support inspection workflows.

5. Data Storage and Security

All user data is transmitted using encrypted communication channels. We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

Data may be stored using secure cloud services including Firebase services for authentication, storage, and database functionality.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

Information may be shared only when necessary to:

  • Deliver application functionality
  • Store and synchronize inspection records
  • Comply with applicable laws or legal obligations
  • Protect security and prevent misuse of the service

7. User Rights

Users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information.

Account and data deletion requests can be submitted through:

https://housing-inspection-2k26.web.app/ https://housing-inspection-2k26.web.app/

8. Data Retention

We retain user data only as long as necessary to provide application services, maintain records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

When a verified deletion request is processed, personal data will be deleted unless retention is required by law, security requirements, or legitimate business obligations.

9. Children's Privacy

Housing Inspection is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
