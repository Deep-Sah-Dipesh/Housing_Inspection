Housing Inspection Account Deletion Request

This page explains how users of the Housing Inspection application can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

Email: rosydipesh70@gmail.com
Subject: Account Deletion Request

Please include the email address associated with your Housing Inspection account.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • User account information
  • Profile details
  • Inspection reports created by the user
  • Inspection photographs uploaded by the user
  • Property-related inspection records
  • Application preferences associated with the account

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained when required for:

  • Legal compliance
  • Dispute resolution
  • Fraud prevention and security purposes

Retention Period

Verified deletion requests are normally processed within 30 days. Any data retained for legal or security reasons will be deleted when retention is no longer required.
